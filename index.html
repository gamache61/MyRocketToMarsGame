Rocket to Mars

buy the parts, build the rocket, beat the board — prototype

Up to 4 teams start with $300,000,000 each. Roll dice, move around the launch site, and buy rocket parts from the tiles you land on — names and prices are marked right on the board. Buy parts in any order you like, whatever tile you land on. Watch out for the four corners: one pays you $25,000,000 for passing, the other three take money for a broken part, stolen goods, and tax — enough to force some real budgeting. Your rocket starts as nothing at all — every part you buy is added to it immediately, right there on screen. Complete your rocket first and you launch immediately, live, on screen.

How to Play Rocket to Mars

Goal: Buy rocket parts before anyone else finishes their build. Finish first and you launch immediately and win. Otherwise, whoever has the most complete rocket after 15 rounds wins.

The board: A rectangular loop that stretches to fill your screen. Each team's pad sits in the middle of one side. On your turn: hit Roll Dice on the Dice tab, move that many spaces, then act. When you're done, hit End Turn right next to it.

Buying: Every ordinary tile always sells one part type — its name and price are shown right on the square. Land on it and, if you can afford it and haven't already collected all the units you need of that part, you can buy one unit — once per turn. Buy parts in any order, whatever tile you land on.

Parts & prices: 🔥 RS-25 Engine $15M (need 4), 🛢️ Core Stage $60M (need 1), 🎯 Solid Rocket Booster $25M (need 2), 🧊 ICPS $40M (need 1), 🛰️ European Service Module $35M (need 1), 🧑‍🚀 Crew Module $30M (need 1), ⚠️ Launch Abort System $25M (need 1). A full rocket costs exactly $300M if you never waste a purchase.

Building: Your rocket starts with nothing showing at all. Every part you buy is added to it right away — you'll see it appear on the Rockets tab as a faint outline that solidifies as you collect enough of that part, until the stage locks in for good.

The four corners: 🚀 Payday — pass or land here and collect $25,000,000. 🔧 Repair Fee — a part breaks and it costs $8,000,000 to ship back and fix. 🚨 Stolen Goods — you're fined $20,000,000 for parts that turned out to be stolen. 🧾 Tax on Goods — a flat $12,000,000 tax.

Launch: Complete all 4 stages and your rocket launches immediately with a full liftoff sequence — game over, you win.
